To download the "Roboto Flex" font from Google:

https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto+Flex:opsz,slnt,wght,GRAD@8..144,-10..0,100..900,-200..150

Then look for the link to the Latin woff2 file.

Axes have to be specified alphabetically in the URL, lowercase axes before uppercase axes.
Range has to be specified for each axis.

Effect of axes and their range are demonstrated at
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto+Flex/tester
and
https://v-fonts.com/fonts/roboto-flex

To download the "Roboto Mono" font from Google:

https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto+Mono:wght@100..700

Then look for the link to the Latin woff2 file.

Effect of axes and their range are demonstrated at
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto+Mono/tester
